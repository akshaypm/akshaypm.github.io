# Signal Brief

Mode: Persuade.

Audience: recruiters and hiring managers for Senior, Staff, and Lead Applied AI, cloud, and backend roles. Job: decide in one sitting whether to download the resume or write. Action: Download resume, or email, or LinkedIn. Proof: Sherpa-v2, then GE provisioning, JLL observability, and Deloitte / McDonald's, then the dated record. Constraints: CV-only claims, no phone on the page, static export, the pinned visual system.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A recruiter's brief, not a template portfolio. The first screen is a left-aligned claim and a resume, refusing the centered hero, the neon stack, and the metric-tile grid.

OWN-WORLD: Paper #F6F3EC, ink #141414, stone #5B5750, hairline #D9D3C7, accent #1B3A6B only on links, focus rings, and the diagram key path. Newsreader, Geist, JetBrains Mono. Sharp corners, no shadow, no cards, no icons.

STORY: In one screen he is a senior applied AI engineer with ten years of production cloud and IoT, now on Sherpa-v2. The reader downloads the resume or writes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark and a four-link nav on a hairline. H1 left, sub line under it, an ink Download resume button with Email and LinkedIn as text. A right-hand margin note, "01 — Now", lists the Sherpa-v2 stack. The resume control is above the fold at 1440×900 and at 390×844.

FORM: Brief-pinned Signal Brief. No concept-seed roll; the approved specification is the direction (seed key: brief-pinned). Signature: the Sherpa signal path, thin ink stations, accent only on the path, drawn in once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
