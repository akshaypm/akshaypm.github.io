# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers hiring for Senior, Staff, or Lead Applied AI, cloud, and backend roles. They are in India or hiring remote for the US, UK, or EU. They skim for about six seconds, then either download the resume or write.

## Product Purpose

A single-page portfolio that makes that reader believe Akshay P M is an Applied AI engineer with ten-plus years of production cloud, IoT, and backend work, and then gives one action: download the resume, or email or LinkedIn him.

## Positioning

He currently builds Sherpa-v2 at Norwin, a GCP Cloud Run platform that deploys and profiles AI-agent workloads. The ten years underneath that are production systems, not a model-research biography.

## Operating Context

Static site, read in a desktop or phone browser, often from a LinkedIn link or a GitHub Pages URL. Success is a resume download or a message. No accounts, no forms, no app state.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One page. Copy traces to the master CV and the current resume. No claim is added.
- Norwin has no public metrics. GE RAG was a development prototype and is omitted here. He integrates MCP as a client and does not publish an MCP server. He is a co-author of Internet-Draft draft-lijo-6lo-expiration-time, which was published as RFC 9034. He is not credited as an RFC author and not as an IEEE author.
- The phone number is not shown on the page. The downloadable resume file is the supplied PDF.
- A public-build slot exists in code and stays unrendered until a public repository is cleared.
- Deploy target is GitHub Pages via static export. Production deploys only from `main`. This redesign does not ship there until merged by a person.

## Brand Commitments

The approved visual world is Signal Brief: editorial, calm, substance first. Paper, ink, one accent, Newsreader, Geist, and JetBrains Mono. Those commitments are binding.

## Evidence on Hand

- Master CV: claims, dates, titles, and the provenance notes for what must never be said.
- Resume PDF, served as `/Akshay_PM_Resume.pdf`.
- No portrait, no customer logos, no Sherpa metrics, no public GitHub repository yet.

## Product Principles

- Proof over atmosphere. A sentence earns its place by naming a system, a decision, or a result that is already true.
- One action, visible before the fold on both a laptop and a phone.
- Silence is part of the claim. What the provenance forbids is absent, not hedged.
- The page should feel written and drawn, not assembled from a component kit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page is public, keyboard usable, and readable without color alone. Target is Lighthouse accessibility of at least 95 on mobile, with visible focus and no motion when the reader asks for reduced motion.
